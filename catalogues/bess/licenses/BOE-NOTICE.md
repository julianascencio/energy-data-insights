# BOE attribution and reuse

Basado en datos de la [Agencia Estatal Boletín Oficial del Estado](https://www.boe.es).

These adapted factual records are neither official publications nor endorsed by
BOE. Original publication/update dates, document identifiers, exact URLs and
checksums are retained in the source register. Administrative authorisation is
not evidence of construction or operation. Battery, hybrid and grid ratings
remain separate; unresolved source arithmetic is explicitly retained.

The [general reuse conditions](https://www.boe.es/informacion/aviso_legal/index.php),
under the Resolution of 27 June 2024, permit commercial and noncommercial reuse
with attribution, preserved meaning/update metadata and no suggestion of
endorsement. This does not apply to excluded third-party works, logos or the
Biblioteca Jurídica Digital. No personal contact details are imported.
