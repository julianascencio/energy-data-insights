# French wind installation registry attribution

Data producers: RTE, Enedis, EDF SEI and ELD, via Open Data Réseaux Énergies.
Source: https://odre.opendatasoft.com/explore/dataset/registre-national-installation-production-stockage-electricite-agrege/
Snapshot: 31 August 2026. Metadata modified 13 October 2026. Retrieved 4 November 2026.

Reuse: Licence Ouverte / Open Licence v2.0, Etalab, April 2017.
https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/ETALAB-Licence-Ouverte-v2.0.pdf
This independent transformed selection is not endorsed by the source producers.

Transformations: complete EOLIE inventory screened; stable EIC installation IDs;
kW divided by 1,000 into separately labeled installed and grid-injection MW;
source dates parsed into ISO dates. No natural-person contacts are included.
All accepted installations remain excluded from capacity totals pending scope
reconciliation. Administrative geography is not converted into point locations.
Original export, metadata and glossary checksums are retained in sources.json.
