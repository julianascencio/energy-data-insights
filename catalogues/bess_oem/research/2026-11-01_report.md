## Research method

Searches covered supplier-hosted product pages, specification PDFs, launch releases
and corporate notices. Manufacturer pages and technical tables were preferred over
aggregator summaries. REPT's specification table required visual PDF inspection.
Envision's page did not expose readable body text; those two records are explicitly
marked as sourced through the search index. OEM announcements are evidence of what
the manufacturer reported, not independent performance validation. The exact URL,
publisher, title, date/revision when available, and field crosswalk are retained.

Records include LFP, NCA, NMC, vanadium flow, iron flow, sodium-ion, aqueous zinc and
sodium-sulfur products. Chemistry is never assigned solely because a supplier uses
it in another product. OEM/integrator identity does not establish cell ownership.

## Findings that changed the data model

1. **Generation and region matter.** Sungrow's 2025 PowerTitan 3.0 launch, preliminary
   ST6900UX-4H sheet and 2026 European 7.14 MWh release have different specifications.
   They remain separate variants. Huawei's URL still contains `215-Series` while
   its current technical page labels `LUNA2000-241-2S1`; the row follows the model
   actually printed in the specification.
2. **Rounded names are not exact ratings.** Samsung's SBB 1.5 technical table says
   5259 kWh while its announcement says 5.26 MWh. REPT's PDF says 6.261 MWh. Sungrow's
   ST225kWh model has a 229 kWh nominal rating. The name and numeric rating remain
   independent fields; no model number is parsed into a specification.
3. **MVA is not MW.** Nidec's configuration matrices and Sungrow's PCS tables report
   apparent power. These are retained as MVA; active power stays null. No assumed
   power factor or inferred discharge duration is introduced.
4. **Families need different handling.** Socomec, mtu and flow-system portfolio pages
   advertise configurable ranges. Those ranges stay nested in family records;
   a maximum power and maximum energy are never fabricated into one purchasable SKU.
5. **Compliance claims have states.** The HiTHIUM 6.25 MWh 4h PDF marks several
   certifications and standards as in progress. That status is preserved. Trina's
   older PDF has an apparent `UL 9450` typo; it is not promoted to a certified claim.
6. **Old products can remain useful research records.** The NAS technical record is
   accompanied by NGK's 31 October 2025 discontinuation notice. Energy Warehouse
   and the 2024 ENDURIUM module are labelled historical; current ESS and Invinity
   families have separate records. A historical product is not a procurement recommendation.
7. **Efficiency is conditional.** OEMs variously quote minimum, maximum, DC, AC or
   unqualified system RTE. Boundary and qualifier columns prevent treating every
   percentage as a comparable round-trip measurement. Unsupported cycle-life and
   zero-degradation marketing claims remain qualified extensions.

The final primary-source pass also adds CATL's 2026 TENER H and TENER Sodium.
The latter is deliberately a family: the June release says more than 30 MWh,
while the August release rounds the integrated system to 30 MWh. International
deliveries are a June 2027 plan in the June source, not confirmed availability.
Tesla Megapack 3/Megablock remain in the technical-datasheet research queue; the
configurator records are not relabelled as the newer generation.

## Reproduce and inspect

```bash
uv run python -m energy_data_retrieval.bess_oem_catalogue validate
uv run python -m energy_data_retrieval.bess_oem_catalogue build --output artifacts/bess-oem-v1
uv run python examples/database/plot_bess_oem_catalogue.py
uv run pytest data-retrieval/tests/test_bess_oem_catalogue.py analysis/tests/test_bess_oem_database.py
```

The initial completeness report is in `2026-11-01_summary.json`; each future snapshot
also reports per-field population and exact input/Parquet SHA-256. No external API
or token is needed to rebuild. The new viewer and downloadable Parquet are published
by the existing documentation workflow after merge and a successful main build.

## Gaps and next research priorities

- Obtain exact current SKUs and full technical sheets for Envision, Canadian Solar,
  Fluence, Eos, Pixii and Wärtsilä. The current Wärtsilä hardware link redirects to
  Valo; document ownership/alias transitions before renaming original IDs. The Valo
  specification links timed out during this pass; no unverified ratings were imported.
- Expand suppliers: CALB, Gotion, CLOU, Kehua, Sineng, Sinexcel, CRRC, Pylontech,
  Toshiba, Hitachi Energy, ABB, Schneider Electric, Alfen, Tesvolt and regional
  Indian, Australian and Latin American integrators. Do not count inverter-only
  offerings as complete BESS without a battery product boundary.
- Add separate cell, module and PCS tables if component-level scope is requested;
  do not mix cell Ah and complete-system MWh as interchangeable equipment.
- Recover missing warranty duty cycles, useful energy at COD/EOL, auxiliary-load
  boundaries, efficiency curves, availability status, manufacturing locations and
  certification documents. Public pages often do not provide these.
- Manufacturer product specifications are not independently tested. Sources can
  change; fixed local records preserve the research result, while external document
  revisions need repeat checks. Exact PDF byte hashes are not available for this pass.
- Prices, inferred project equipment links, rankings, fabricated cell suppliers and
  broad sales-region claims are excluded. Project inventories are not modified.
