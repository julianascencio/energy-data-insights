### Projects

The checksum-pinned DESNZ file contains 14,657 entries of all technologies,
including 7,460 explicitly classified `Solar Photovoltaics`. The selected 336
records were screened against **all** those PV entries, including other regions,
non-operating applications and later/revised applications. The register's OGL v3
declaration was checked on the official publication metadata, and a DESNZ
attribution notice was added. Raw downloaded CSV bytes stay outside Git.

All 189 accepted projects have reported capacity; 149 also have site area.
Source names, administrative locations, planning references, native grid points,
record-update dates and operational milestones are retained where available.
`MWelec` is stored as capacity with unspecified AC/DC basis; native coordinates
are not transformed into asserted WGS84 points. Registry operational dates are
milestones in extensions, not inferred commissioning dates. Status is explicitly
the historical registry observation as of 30 June 2026, not a claim of inspection
or continuous operation on the retrieval date. Applicant/operator fields and
private addresses were omitted.

Name/punctuation matches, shared planning references, reapplication links,
phase/alias/hybrid names, nearby native-grid points, missing coordinates and
sub-MW privacy concerns were held. Screening never merges records. The full
accepted-ID ledger, every withheld ID and its reason, source hash and processed
partitions are in `20261101T0856_repd_review.json`. There were no prior GBR project
records. Existing and incoming canonical IDs/names were checked before acceptance.
The proximity screen is conservative, not proof that every distant application
represents a distinct plant; future evidence may require an explicit crosswalk.

REPD has changing historical inclusion thresholds (1 MW before 2021, 150 kW
thereafter). This is neither a complete UK census nor worldwide project coverage.
