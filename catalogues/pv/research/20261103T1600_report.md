# PV research — 3 November 2026, Atlantic/Canary

Continued open PV PR #103. Screened six deferred ARENA portfolio lines
against AEMO's pinned October 2026 workbook and independent ARENA or
owner profiles. Accepted Kidston Phase One (KSP1), White Rock (WRSF1),
Oakey Phase One (OAKEY1SF), Collinsville PV (CSPVPS1) and Emu Downs.
Normanton remains deferred. Separate AEMO hydro, wind, battery and later
solar rows were not assigned to the PV plants. Western Australia's Emu
Downs uses APA owner evidence, with AC/DC basis and commissioning year
unset. Historical total project costs include financing.

Kidston DC remains unset: AEMO row 830 reports 60 MW DC and Genex
reports 63 MW DC. AEMO labels the tracking category Fixed while Genex
reports single-axis; neither was promoted. First energisation is not
a commercial commissioning date. White Rock's 2017 brochure reports
AUD 5.4m grant, while the earlier announcement reports AUD 6m:
https://arena.gov.au/news/whiterock-solar-farm-secures-6-million-arena-funding/
The accepted value is explicitly a 2017 source observation, not a
claim of final disbursement.

Three exact MidNite 1000 V combiner enclosure models were accepted:
MNPV2-1000, MNPV4-1000 and MNPV10-1000. Fuse holders are separate;
ten holders do not imply ten strings in the transformerless setup.
The Rev D manual specifies ten fuse holders for the base MNPV12,
whereas the earlier specification reports eight fused strings.
No base-model field was changed. Antaisolar still lacks a reconciled
AT-Spark exact revision for comparison.

Five new projects and three new combiners; no existing records enriched,
new country/manufacturer or canonical quarantine. End-2025 IEA PVPS
ranking unchanged. Discovery and reconciliation ran roughly three minutes
from 15:56, then validation and publication. Yield was limited by the
remaining portfolio partition and exact enclosure scope.
