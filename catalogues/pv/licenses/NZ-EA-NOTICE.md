# New Zealand Electricity Authority attribution

Selected facts from the Electricity Authority, on behalf of the Crown, existing
dispatched generation plant dataset (2 July 2026) are used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source: <https://www.emi.ea.govt.nz/Wholesale/Datasets/Generation/GenerationFleet/Existing/20260702_DispatchedGenerationPlant.csv>

Copyright statement: <https://www.ea.govt.nz/copyright-disclaimer-transparency/>

Changes: selected explicitly photovoltaic entries, reviewed identities, normalized
units and dates, omitted unrelated technologies and preserved effective-date and
capacity-basis limitations. This catalogue is not endorsed by the Authority.
The source is periodically updated on a best-endeavours basis, not a real-time
operating inventory. No logos, imagery or third-party submissions are reproduced.
