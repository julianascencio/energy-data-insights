# Chile CNE capacity data attribution

Source: Comisión Nacional de Energía (Chile), **Capacidad Instalada**, resource
**Capacidad Instalada de Gx**, published through [Datos Abiertos](https://datos.gob.cl/dataset/capacidad-instalada).
The catalogue entry identifies Creative Commons Attribution (`cc-by`) and links
its [licence entry](http://www.opendefinition.org/licenses/cc-by); it does not
identify a numbered version. The pinned CSV is dated 16 March 2026 in its
`fecha_act` column and was last modified on the portal on 1 April 2026.

The catalogue retains a selected, transformed subset of factual PV observations.
Locale-specific MW values and dates are normalized, gross and net MW remain
separate, and rows are linked only to manually reconciled existing projects.
Personal/tax details, organization strings, coordinates and the raw CSV are
omitted. See the exact source URL, checksum, row locators and limitations in
`sources.json` and the dated CNE review. No present operation, AC/DC conversion
or commercial operation date is inferred. This notice applies only to the CNE
CSV and does not establish rights for the separate Ministry GIS or BCN layer.
