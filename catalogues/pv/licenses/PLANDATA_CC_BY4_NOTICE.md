# Plandata municipal planning metadata

Contains modified/extracted municipal planning metadata from Plandata.dk,
Plan- og Landdistriktsstyrelsen and the originating municipalities, CC BY4.0.
https://www.plandata.dk/webservices
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Extraction and normalization are performed by this catalogue; no endorsement is
implied. Retrieval and individual source-update dates remain in source records
and observations. Administrative plans do not establish current plant operation,
commissioning, generating-unit identities or whole-project capacity boundaries.

The declaration explicitly covers municipal planning layers and layers owned by
the agency. It is not automatically applied to third-party installation layers,
externally linked planning documents, artwork or private information. No contact
fields, individual owners or original planning documents are redistributed.
