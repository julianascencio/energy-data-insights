# Madrid municipal photovoltaic inventory attribution

Source: Ayuntamiento de Madrid, Dirección General de Sostenibilidad y Control Ambiental,
[Inventario de instalaciones fotovoltaicas](https://datos.madrid.es/dataset/300466-0-instalaciones-fotovoltaicas).
CSV filename3July2026, temporal coverage through30June2026; retrieved3November2026UTC.

[CC BY4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as declared in the
[municipal reuse conditions](https://datos.madrid.es/pages/condiciones-de-uso).
No endorsement by the Ayuntamiento is implied. The publisher retains source rights.

Transformations: selected distinct reviewed municipal PV sites; decimal-comma kWp
converted to MWdc; source service dates parsed; native building point coordinates
retained with unspecified datum/accuracy. No plant coordinates derived from city
centres. Building IDs are not national plant IDs. Repeated/nearby/missing identity
and invalid-coordinate observations remain held. Undefined surface is not promoted
to land/array area. Street addresses and installer labels omitted; no ownership,
operator, EPC, installed equipment or present-operation inference. Full raw CSV,
documentation and binary snapshots remain outside Git. Source hashes, exact URLs,
logical sequence/building locators and dated decisions are retained in research.
