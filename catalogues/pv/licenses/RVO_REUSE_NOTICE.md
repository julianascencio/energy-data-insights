# RVO reuse and attribution

Publisher: Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland (RVO).
Source: SDE(+)(+) Projecten in beheer, peildatum juli 2026.

- Source landing: https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/sde/aanvragen/feiten-en-cijfers
- Source workbook: https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-09/SDE-projecten-in-beheer-juli-2026.xlsx
- Permission: https://www.rvo.nl/onderwerpen/open-data

The publisher explicitly allows reuse of its open data and directly links the SDE
viewer. Attribution is voluntary unless a dataset-specific condition says otherwise;
RVO is credited here. This is not a claim that another dataset's CC0 declaration
applies to this workbook. Image material is excluded from the permission.

Changes: checksum verification, explicit PV classification, anonymised-row exclusion,
private-column omission, ground/water partitioning, exact-reference cursor and native
financial/lifecycle evidence staging. The workbook's disclaimer is retained: no legal
entitlement follows from its contents. Anonymised people/partnerships are not identified
or joined to other datasets. No applicant/address/postcode data, images, complete
workbook or inferred plant identities/ratings are redistributed in this catalogue.
No RVO endorsement or current-operation verification is implied.
