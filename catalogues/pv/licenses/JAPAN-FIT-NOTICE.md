This repository's review candidates are independently extracted, translated,
filtered and reformatted from the Tottori prefecture workbook, snapshot
30 September 2026, published 14 October 2026. They are not an official government
product. The portal warns that paper filings reflect 30 June 2026 and certification
may not reflect expired plans yet. A certification is not evidence of current
operation. Person names, contacts, business addresses and parcel addresses are
omitted from published review output. Raw workbooks are not redistributed.

These are held research candidates, not accepted project records. The source
register preserves exact URLs, retrieval timestamps and downloaded-byte checksums.
