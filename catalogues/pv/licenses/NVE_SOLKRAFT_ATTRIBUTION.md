# Norway NVE Solkraft attribution

Source: Norwegian Water Resources and Energy Directorate (NVE), *Solkraft*,
layer 2 (*Solkraft_konsesjonsbehandling*), accessed 7 November 2026.
The [NVE map catalogue](https://kartkatalog.nve.no/om) identifies NVE map data as
distributed under the Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data (NLOD).
[NVE's map service documentation](https://api.nve.no/doc/web-map-service-wms/)
describes the open dataset access and requires source credit. See the
[original map service](https://kart.nve.no/enterprise/rest/services/Solkraft/MapServer)
and the exact requests and hashes in the source register.

This catalogue transforms a bounded selection of source facts into individual
evidence contexts and links them to separately reviewed existing PV identities.
There is no NVE endorsement. Original map geometry, owner/contact data, and
copies of individual case documents are excluded. Forecasts, proposed capacity,
grid allocations, and operating ratings retain their separate scopes.
