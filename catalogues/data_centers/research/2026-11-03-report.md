## Boundary decisions

AirTrunk pages display total location/campus capacities with `+` qualifiers and
do not identify the electrical boundary. All 22 are stored as reported
lower-bound observations. They do not populate IT, grid or facility capacity
columns and cannot enter viewer capacity sums. Portfolio listing alone does not
establish dated operation, so lifecycle is `unknown`. Design PUE observations
retain exact/less-than qualifiers and null measurement periods.

Vertiv voltage ranges are observations, not nominal voltages, and no kW is
calculated from voltage/current. NVIDIA accelerator limits remain per-card
maximum power/TDP, distinct from server input and rack power. Tesla P4's
unresolved 50 W / 75 W choice was excluded from normalized power.

Africa Data Centres' explicitly labelled client/critical IT loads populate IT
capacity only with their stated design or ultimate basis. NBO1's 37.5 MW secured
utility capacity is the first normalized grid-capacity observation. Generic
`power`, `site capacity` and Accra `MW` figures whose electrical boundary is not
disclosed remain observations. JHB1's 14.5 MW facility-sheet value and current
page's 20 MW expansion value are quarantined pending a dated phase boundary.
Forecast expansion dates are not treated as completion evidence. Operator-claimed
certification badges remain unnormalized until issuer-registry verification.

ODATA QR03's 300 MW is ultimate IT capacity and its 72 MW is the first-building
operating boundary; both remain on an aggregate campus record excluded from
capacity sums. The separately reported 200 MW energization and 400 MW expansion
stay observations because the source does not identify them as IT, grid
connection or total-facility demand. Its 275,000 m2 country-sheet value conflicts
with 275 m2 rendered on the dated launch page, so total area is quarantined.
QR03 and SP04's combined US$630 million cooling investment is not allocated.

The R134a adapter uses the OEM's printed airflow N/A row as the availability mask;
it does not fill blanks from R410A or synthesize refrigerant/compressor variants.
Antel's final-capacity statement is explicitly greater than 1,000 racks, so it is
retained as a lower-bound observation. Tigo's 260-rack site capability, 98/220
corporate allocations and 380/770 m2 initial/ultimate technical areas remain
separately scoped observations. Uptime Institute pages corroborate both facility
identities and Tier III award scopes, but `certifications_json` stays null because
public issue dates were unavailable.

Meta's portfolio establishes campus identities but not current lifecycle or
building/phase boundaries, so all 33 records remain `unknown` lifecycle campus
aggregates and are excluded from facility capacity sums. A displayed break-ground
year is not expanded into a full construction date. No electrical capacity is
derived from investment or location text.

PowerUPS 6000 kVA stays apparent power: shared unity output power factor is not
used to calculate active kW. Voltage and frequency options remain observations;
grouped dimensions and the 92–97% family efficiency span remain null at exact
configuration level. Trinergy CE active outputs preserve the OEM table's 40 C
condition. Its up-to efficiency claims remain observations because load conditions
are undisclosed; battery voltage/chemistry do not establish energy or runtime.
