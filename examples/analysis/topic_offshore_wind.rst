Offshore wind economics
=======================

Assess CAPEX escalation and financing-rate sensitivity using a published
Japanese industry planning benchmark and explicitly illustrative scenarios.
Also quantify hypothetical transmission-energy exposure from export cable
failure while keeping construction incidents distinct from operational losses.
Add a separate vessel-installation and grid-readiness critical-path sensitivity
using published Dogger Bank C project scope and synthetic daily conditions.
